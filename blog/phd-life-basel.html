PhD Life in Basel: Science at the Border

Basel is a city of layers—historic, cultural, and deeply scientific. Positioned at the junction of Switzerland, France, and Germany, it is the perfect microcosm for a PhD in Biomedical Engineering.

The Academic Atmosphere

Working at the University of Basel means being part of a centuries-old tradition that has evolved into a global hub for Life Sciences. My days are split between the computational rigor of MRI physics and the collaborative environment of the Department of Biomedical Engineering.

Living at the Tri-Border

One of the most unique aspects of life here is the internationality. You can have breakfast in Switzerland, lunch in France, and dinner in Germany—all within a short tram ride. This fluid movement across borders mirrors the interdisciplinary nature of my research, where physics, computer science, and medicine meet.

Between long shimming sessions and data analysis, the Rhine river provides a constant backdrop for reflection. Basel isn't just a place to study; it's a place that challenges you to think beyond the lab.
